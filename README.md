A program (TypeScript, Node 22, standard library only) that writes one
warm family scene to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> Every year my little brother saves the last bite of his birthday cake for Dad, and Dad — who is allergic to cake — still eats it.

129 characters, 133 bytes, no trailing newline.

Why this line: it is the only candidate of eight that wins on the judge
brief's first three criteria at once — a real family on the page, warm
enough to make a proud judge smile; heartfelt, with two small acts of love
(the brother saves the last bite, Dad eats it anyway); and funny in a
light, recognisable way (Dad is allergic to cake). The detail is specific
and fresh — the last bite, the birthday cake, the allergy, every year —
not a greeting-card line. The twist lands in the second half: the love
runs both ways.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

The output is deterministic: two runs write byte-identical files.
